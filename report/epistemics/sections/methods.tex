\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Source strata and models}
The frozen cohort contains 120 distinct source groups, 30 per domain. Finance
uses 15 FinQA and 15 numerical TAT-QA cases. Legal reasoning uses ten
ContractNLI cases per label: Entailment, Contradiction and NotMentioned.
Science uses ten SciFact cases per mapped reference class: Supported,
Contradicted and InsufficientEvidence. The last class means no annotated
support in the supplied evidence, not a universal judgment of scientific
truth. Education uses 30 distinct MathDial problems: 15 original erroneous
student workings and 15 controls replacing the working with the reference
solution. We score the numerical solution, not pedagogical quality.

Fixed hash ordering selects cases before inspecting model outputs; prior pilot
agreements are excluded. All records and controls were checked against pinned
originals. Group identities and operational settings are in
Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}. Domain balance is not employment weighting.

The answering models are DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813, GLM-5.3, Kimi K2.6,
GPT-OSS-120B and Inkling 2 Small, all through native Cloudflare endpoints.
These are five families with different capabilities, not five equivalent
frontier systems. Every model answers every case once before Veracity is
mentioned. It supplies an answer, explanation, quotations and a scalar
probability of correctness. Exact visible responses are frozen.

\subsection{Branches, forecasts and executed verdicts}
Every interpretable answer receives four objections and defence seeds
101--105, yielding 12,000 assigned branches before missing initial outputs.
Review requests reconsideration; pressure asserts error without evidence;
the false-alternative arm proposes an explicitly reference-wrong answer using
a scripted invalid rationale; audit requests checking operations, units,
scope and exceptions, with gold source spans for ContractNLI when available.
The false alternatives are controlled errors, not independently validated
naturalistic objections. Every branch retains the same source material, and
all defence prompts warn that disagreement alone is not evidence of error.

After Veracity is disclosed, the model reports its current answer, declared
stance and whether it still supports the original. It separately forecasts
the original answer's correctness and jury acceptance. A standardized wrapper
publishes the original answer as supported by the material; this wrapper is
authored by the harness and is not evidence of model-authored rhetorical
overstatement. The defence is frozen before jurors run. The common panel
contains three separate GPT-OSS calls with source, original claim, objection
and defence. Identity, reference grades, peer ballots and all probability
fields are withheld. Votes execute through actual Rust/HTTP registration,
commitment, reveal and Solidity/Anvil settlement, at fixed 1$\times$ synthetic
stakes. A predeclared rotating subset replays one frozen branch per case
with three DeepSeek jurors; it never regenerates the defence.

The cloud journal preserves failed observations without replacement. Role
budgets are fixed across models; Kimi uses high reasoning and the others low.
Lossless representation normalization preserves raw failures and parser hashes.

\subsection{Grades, endpoints and uncertainty}
\label{sec:uncertainty}
Literal grades use the frozen numerical adapter or categorical reference.
An explicitly post hoc, versioned source-audit sensitivity additionally checks
eight numerical cases (Section~\ref{sec:grading}). It does not alter
assignment, source material or model outputs. We preserve both grade sets;
neither constitutes independent expert adjudication.

For an original correctness indicator $C$, a prospective probability $p$ is
scored by the Brier loss $(p-C)^2$. Defence forecasts of source correctness
and jury acceptance have separate binary targets. A concession is a
reference-correct original followed by an incorrect current answer; a
correction is a reference-incorrect original followed by a correct answer.
Declared support is recorded separately from answer changes. Verdicts concern
the frozen original, including after a correction. Missing answers and
unsettled panels have explicit denominators and are never recoded as beliefs.

We use 2,000 percentile bootstrap resamples of whole source groups within the
original sampling strata. Repeated seeds remain inside their source group.
Paired arm contrasts first match defence seeds, then average differences
within source. We also report sample SD across the five seed-level aggregate
rates, using denominator four when all five are available. Bootstrap intervals
describe the selected case distribution under source-group resampling;
collapsed intervals at observed boundaries do not imply population certainty.
Provider seed controls do not prove independent generations.
