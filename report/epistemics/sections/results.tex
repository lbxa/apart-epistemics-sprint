\subsection{Stable answers can preserve mismatches}
All 12,000 primary assignments are accounted for: 11,620 execute and 380 are
unavailable because 19 initial outputs cannot be interpreted. The 581 usable
initials include 29 lossless normalizations. There are 11,483 valid defences
and 11,469 settled verdicts; 151 executed panels abort. Missing outputs remain
in completion denominators (Appendix~\ref{app:sector-completion}).

Table~\ref{tab:sector-headline} separates initial agreement, answer revision,
declared support and jury acceptance under the audited sensitivity. Pressure
loses 20/2,543 matching originals (0.8\%). Stability also preserves
mismatches: audit repairs 17/334 (5.1\%), while 314/334 replies still support
their original. These pooled counts describe the cohort, not independent
documents or a general model ranking.

Repair rates differ by domain. Legal audit
repairs 11/153 mismatches, versus 3/158 under review. For GPT-OSS, the paired
source-level difference is 13.3 percentage points, with a pointwise 95\%
bootstrap interval [0.0, 31.1]; nine error sources support that contrast.
Scientific mismatches receive zero repairs in 579 valid defences across all
arms, yet 539/570 settled panels uphold their originals. The 145
defence generations in a fully completed error arm arise from just seven distinct
scientific source groups. Annotation scope is therefore a serious alternative
to interpreting them as factual errors. Appendices~\ref{app:sector-behavior}--\ref{app:sector-seeds}
give domain figures and seed SDs; Appendix~\ref{app:intermediate-evidence}
provides the full intermediate evidence ledger.

\subsection{Confidence depends on the task and scoring target}

Figure~\ref{fig:sector-confidence} compares prospective confidence with both
grade sets. Mean confidence exceeds reference accuracy for four of five legal
strata and all five scientific strata. For example, Kimi's legal answers match
18/26 references (69.2\%) while its mean stated confidence is 95.2\%.
Inkling's legal accuracy instead exceeds confidence: 86.7\% versus 84.2\%.
Kimi's legal defence Brier losses are 0.043 for jury acceptance and 0.229 for
source correctness. Different base rates and eligible sets prevent treating
this as a direct honesty comparison (Table~\ref{tab:sector-forecasts}). These
small selected strata do not establish a general model ordering.

\subsection{A false defence changes observed verification}
\label{sec:grading}
The numerical audit changes 30 initial grades (Table~\ref{tab:grading}), so
reference mismatches alone cannot establish factual error. An exact product
is less ambiguous: GLM states $1084\times108.51=117628.84$, maintains it after
audit, and receives three supporting GPT-OSS votes. The correct result is
117624.84.

A separately declared post hoc diagnostic uses five seed blocks of three
standalone calls per condition and jury family. Both families calculate the
bare product correctly in 15/15 calls. Without the defence, DeepSeek rejects
the false claim in 15/15 and GPT-OSS in 14/15. Adding the frozen defence
reduces correct rejections to 3/15 and 0/15. Appending a checked decomposition
restores 15/15 for both. Four usable checked-author replies also correct the
product and withdraw the original; one fails. These are one-source diagnostic
results, not additional Veracity settlements or a general effect estimate
(Appendix~\ref{app:arithmetic}).
