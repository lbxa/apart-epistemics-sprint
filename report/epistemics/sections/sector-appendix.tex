\section{Completion and conditional denominators}
\label{app:sector-completion}
Each model is assigned 30 initial cases and 600 branches per domain: four
conditions and five defence seeds. A missing initial answer makes 20 branches
structurally unavailable. A completed defence can remain usable for revision
analysis even when the jury has no settled verdict. Table~\ref{tab:sector-coverage}
keeps these stages distinct. Missing outputs are not classified as false beliefs.

 including structurally unavailable branches.}
 \label{tab:sector-coverage}
\end{table}

\section{Revision and jury support across domains}
\label{app:sector-behavior}
All rates in this section use the source-audited sensitivity; literal grades
remain in the portable snapshot and \path{sector-metrics.csv}. A loss of
reference agreement is not automatically an unjustified retreat. The legal
and scientific disagreements require independent adjudication. Intervals are
pointwise 95\% percentile bootstrap intervals from 2,000 source-group resamples
within the original sampling strata. They are exploratory, not simultaneous
confidence bands. A collapsed boundary interval cannot rule out failures in
unseen cases. Initial-mismatch strata differ between models, so conditional
rates do not establish a general model ranking.

\section{Confidence ranking and average calibration}
\label{app:confidence-ranking}
Confidence AUC is the observed probability that a reference-matching answer
receives a higher confidence than a mismatching answer, counting ties as half.
It describes ordering, while mean confidence minus accuracy describes average
calibration. AUC is undefined when either outcome class is absent. These
descriptive AUC values are exploratory and have no reported confidence
interval; some strata contain only one error.

\begin{table}[htbp]
 \centering\small\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
 \input{tables/sector-confidence-diagnostics}
 \caption{Initial confidence diagnostics under the audited sensitivity.
 Errors are mismatches over valid outputs. Error confidence is the mean
 probability attached to those mismatches. The gap and its source-group
 bootstrap interval use percentage points. Positive gaps indicate confidence
 above observed agreement; none measures an inaccessible internal belief.}
 \label{tab:confidence-ranking}
\end{table}

\section{Cross-family jury replay}
\label{app:judge-sensitivity}
The rotating assignment selects one seed-101 branch per source, with author
and condition determined by the frozen case index. Of 120 selections, seven
lack an available defence. All 113 eligible replays execute; four DeepSeek
panels abort, leaving 109 paired settled verdicts. The panels agree in 108/109
pairs. Both uphold ten original reference mismatches; DeepSeek additionally
upholds one legal mismatch that GPT-OSS rejects. Thus high cross-family
agreement does not resolve reference correctness. The replay is too small and
unevenly crossed to estimate a general judge-family effect.
