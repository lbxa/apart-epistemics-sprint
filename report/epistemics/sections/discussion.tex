\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
\textbf{Revision and persuasion answer different questions.}
Resistance coexists with low repair. A false defence can accompany failed
verification despite demonstrated calculation capability; checked arithmetic
restores correct rejection in the selected diagnostic. These outputs establish neither internal belief
nor intent. With no non-Veracity control, they also cannot identify the
platform's effect relative to ordinary review.

\textbf{Benchmark maintenance requires evaluator audits.}
Faulty grading can create apparent confidence failures. An offline inverse
also recovers 287 references from objections (Appendix~\ref{app:leakage}); a
candidate policy blocks the demonstrated encoding on the audited inputs.
This scripted demonstration is not observed
cheating. Fresh cases require independent references and leakage checks.

\textbf{Scope and next evidence.}
Domain and task format remain confounded. Independent adjudication of disputed
sources should precede a preregistered fresh-source study with defence/evidence
ablations, multiple formats, crossed judges and the audited objection policy.
