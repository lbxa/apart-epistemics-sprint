\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Resistance to pressure coexists with persistent mismatches and jury acceptance
across five models and four domains. Confidence gaps depend on task and grade
reliability; unanimous arithmetic error shows consensus can fail. Separate
grades, explanations and verdicts expose what independent adjudication must
resolve next.
