\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this five-model, four-domain cohort, resistance to pressure coexists with
persistent reference mismatches and jury acceptance of those mismatches.
Confidence gaps depend on the task and grading policy. A directly checked
arithmetic case shows that unanimous acceptance can preserve a false claim.
Measuring reference agreement, declared support and verdicts separately makes
these failures inspectable and identifies where independent adjudication is needed.
