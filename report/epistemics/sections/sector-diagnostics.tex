\section{Intermediate evidence and complete readouts}
\label{app:intermediate-evidence}
The headline figures compress several stages: selecting source material,
obtaining an interpretable answer, eliciting a defence, retaining its support
decision, and executing a verdict. This appendix exposes those intermediate
stages, including ordinary successes, zeros, failures and uncertain contrasts.
The additional diagnostics are exploratory analyses of the same frozen
outputs. No new model generation or semantic evaluator is introduced, and no
failed output is replaced.

We denote a scalar or label reference match by $M$ and a mismatch by $X$.
Grades use the source-audited sensitivity unless explicitly
marked literal (L). The distinction does not certify every reference as truth.
In particular, a correct final number does not validate all accompanying
explanatory claims. All intervals retain source groups, use the original
sampling strata and are pointwise rather than simultaneous. Repeated seeds,
conditions and ballots do not create independent source documents.

categorical and correct-working adaptations remain those described in

exports. A catalog field saying an imported task is not integrated does not

\subsection{All paired contrasts against neutral review}
\label{app:diagnostic-paired}
Raw percentages can change because different generations complete. The
contrasts below use matching seeds, then average within each eligible source
before resampling sources. A positive concession contrast means more matching
originals are lost; a positive correction contrast means more mismatches are
repaired. Small error strata can yield a collapsed interval even for a
nonzero difference. Such an interval describes this empirical resampling
distribution and does not establish population certainty or a significant
general treatment effect. No multiplicity-adjusted hypothesis test is claimed.

\subsection{Confidence bins and near-certain mismatches}
\label{app:diagnostic-confidence}
Mean confidence and a Brier loss compress the distribution of probabilities.
Figure~\ref{fig:diagnostic-reliability} and
Table~\ref{tab:diagnostic-reliability} retain ten fixed-width probability bins.
The final bin includes 1.0; earlier bins include their left endpoint and
exclude their right. Some bins are empty or contain very few sources. Labels
and intervals make that sparsity visible. This is a descriptive view of
the selected task mixture, not a calibrated deployment policy.

Across usable initials, 41 of 487 outputs at confidence at least 0.9 mismatch
the audited reference; five of 112 outputs at confidence exactly 1.0 do so.
Table~\ref{tab:diagnostic-high-confidence} retains the model/domain breakdown.
An error fraction conditional on high confidence differs from the incidence
of high-confidence errors among all usable answers.

\subsection{Stance, support and replacement grades}
\label{app:diagnostic-support}
No valid reply declares \emph{maintain} while reporting that the original is
unsupported; no repaired mismatch still reports support for the old answer.
These zero-count cross-checks are part of the result. They do not make the
fields interchangeable. Twenty-three replies withdraw support while their
current scalar or label remains reference-matching. An author can qualify
an incomplete explanation while retaining its correct number, as the rope
example below illustrates. Conversely, a retained mismatch can remain
confidently supported. Table~\ref{tab:diagnostic-support} reports the full

\subsection{Literal quotations and inference remain separate}
\label{app:diagnostic-citations}
Eight hundred forty-six defences preserve a reference mismatch while
providing at least one quotation and no literal mismatch; 698 of the 837
settled originals in that subset are upheld. Exact copying therefore can
coexist with reference disagreement. This does not establish that quotations
caused jury acceptance or that every reference disagreement is an invalid
inference. A literal mismatch can also be a paraphrase or punctuation change.
The diagnostic normalizes whitespace and searches the supplied task and
document; it performs no semantic entailment judgment.

\subsection{Reported resources and failure receipts}

standalone diagnostic calls. Lossless initial interpretations are not counted
as additional model calls. Only role, model, phase, status, reported token
usage, finish reason, safe error classification and source locators enter the
metadata annex; response/error bodies and private SDK reasoning are excluded.

role, as described in Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}. Token counts do not identify reasoning
quality, and no inference-speed ranking is made. The request timing and queue
latency needed to isolate speed are not present in the admitted projection.

\subsection{Visible traces selected by outcome pattern}
\label{app:diagnostic-traces}
These six examples use a reproducible post hoc selection rule: the lowest
eligible branch ID in each observed pattern, requiring three valid ballots,
excluding the five sources already illustrated and earlier selected IDs.
The global pattern counts can overlap and count generations, not independent
sources. Exact contiguous prefixes are displayed with explicit ellipses;
field offsets, whole-field and excerpt hashes, row hashes and parent file
hashes remain in \path{sector-diagnostic-inputs.json}. They are visible model
statements, not private SDK reasoning or observations of internal cognition.

\textbf{Ordinary support.} The neutral-review pattern includes 2,420 replies
that retain a matching answer, support it and receive an upheld verdict.
The first example shows a simple calculation working as intended.
\input{tables/diagnostic-trace-1}

\textbf{Verification can reject a mismatch.} There are 216 settled replies
that retain a reference mismatch and receive rejection. This example preserves
the disputed scope rather than treating every confident defence as persuasive.
\input{tables/diagnostic-trace-2}

\textbf{Pressure can produce a reference loss.} The twenty pressure losses
include a different model on the same SciFact claim. Its narrower reading
requires adjudication; changing labels does not by itself establish gullibility.
\input{tables/diagnostic-trace-3}

\textbf{Author repair and jury acceptance can diverge.} Two repaired originals
are upheld. The jury judges the saved original; an improved replacement does
not retroactively validate it. The visible legal interpretation remains open
to independent review.
\input{tables/diagnostic-trace-4}

\textbf{A matching original can be rejected.} There are 309 instances where
both original and replacement match the reference but the jury rejects the
original. The selected consultant-disclosure example makes the inference
disagreement inspectable rather than inferring a jury error solely from a label.
\input{tables/diagnostic-trace-5}

\textbf{The scalar endpoint can miss a useful qualification.} Twenty-three
